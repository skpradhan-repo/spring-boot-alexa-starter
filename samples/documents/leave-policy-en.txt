Leave Policy

All permanent employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave each year. Leave is credited on the first day of January.

Sick leave requires a doctor's certificate if the absence is longer than three days. Employees must inform their manager on the first day of absence.

Unused annual leave, up to 10 days, can be carried over to the next year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave and must be taken within six months of the birth.
