# Expense Policy

## Travel

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 8,000 rupees per night in metro cities.

## Claims

Submit every claim within 30 days of the expense, with the original receipts attached. Claims above 50,000 rupees need approval from a department head.

## Meals

Daily meal allowance during travel is 1,500 rupees. Alcohol is never reimbursed.
